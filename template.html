Good to know

Questions UAE businesses ask before getting in touch

Do you take commissions from Microsoft, Google or anyone else?

No. We take no commission or kickback from any vendor. You pay us for the advice and the work; the tools are passed through to you at cost. That's the whole point of being independent — it's the only way our recommendation can be genuinely about you.

Do we own our own accounts and domains?

Yes, always. Every licence, domain and account we set up is registered in your name, under your control. If you ever decide to work with someone else, everything stays with you and we help you hand it over cleanly.

What size of business do you work with?

Small offices and growing SMEs — roughly one to fifty people. We're built for teams that need the judgement of an IT department without the cost of hiring one.

Do we have to take all three areas?

Not at all. Come to us with one challenge — adopting AI, choosing hardware and software, or sorting out your website and email — or with all three. We scope to what you actually need.

Where in the UAE do you work?

We're based in Sharjah and work with businesses across the UAE — Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman and Abu Dhabi. Most of the review and recommendation work happens remotely; setup and rollout can be done on site where it helps.

What does it cost to work with you?

The first conversation is free. After that you pay a visible advisory or project fee, and any hardware and software is passed through to you at cost with the invoice shown. No markup, no commission, no bundled pricing that hides where the money goes.

How are you different from an IT support company?

An IT company usually sells you something — a licence, a device, a managed contract — and earns on what it sells. We earn only on the advice and the work, so we can tell you when the cheaper option is the better one, or when you don't need to buy anything at all.

You're new. Why should we trust you?

Fair question. We'd rather you judge us on how we work than on a logo wall. The first conversation costs nothing, everything we set up is owned by you, and our pricing is transparent by design. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you.
